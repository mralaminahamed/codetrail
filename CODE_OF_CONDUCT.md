# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever
their background or identity. We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

The full text of the covenant, including the standards of behaviour and the enforcement guidelines,
is at the link above and applies to all project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@mralaminahamed](https://github.com/mralaminahamed),
on GitHub. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled in confidence.
